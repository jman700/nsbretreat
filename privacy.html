Your cookie choice

When you first visit, a banner asks what you allow. Nothing in the optional groups runs until you say yes, and you can change your mind at any time.

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, we treat it as "Reject all" and do not show the banner. We ask again after 13 months.

Services we use

  • Weather, wave and tide figures come from Open-Meteo and NOAA. Our own server fetches them, so those services do not see your address.
